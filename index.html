Anshuman Sinha

  Welcome to my personal website! I am currently a second-year doctoral student at the Université Paris Cité in France, working on differentiable shape models of cells in young embryos — both static and dynamic. I am fortunate to be supervised by Dr. Hervé Turlier and surrounded by a truly interdisciplinary work environment at the prestigious Collège de France in the heart of the city.

  Right before, in 2024, I completed my Masters (French M2) in the popular Applied Mathematics and Computer Vision (MVA — Mathématiques, Vision, Apprentissage) programme enrolled at Institut Polytechnique de Paris.

  Previously, I have worked as a Research Assistant at the Biomedical Imaging Group (BIG) in EPFL for a duration of 6 months. Primarily advised by Prof. Michael Unser and Dr. Julien Fageot, I devised a stochastic optimization algorithm to estimate the unknown 3D rotation of a known template embedded in a discrete 3D image, using spherical harmonics and steerable filters, in a computationally efficient manner.

  My undergraduate thesis work was carried out at Dr. Virginie Uhlmann 's outgoing lab at the European Bioinformatics Institute EMBL-EBI in Cambridge, UK. The yearlong project explored novel avenues to modify and apply the latest developments in deep Reinforcement Learning to contour-fitting problems using spline models on cell segmentation data.

  Thereafter, in 2022, I graduated from the National Institute of Technology (NITK) in Karnataka, India, with a Bachelors in Electrical Engineering and a minor in Information Technology, with a full grade for my thesis work.
